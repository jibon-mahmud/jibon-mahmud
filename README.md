### About

I'm a **Web Analyst and Performance Marketer** who makes ad data trustworthy. I lead a five-person team covering analytics, web development and digital marketing, and I personally handle every tracking build.

Most ad accounts lose conversions to iOS privacy changes, ad blockers and broken browser pixels. I rebuild that data layer with **server-side tracking**, so Meta, TikTok and Google optimize for real buyers instead of guesses.
